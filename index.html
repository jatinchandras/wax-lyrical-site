AThe shop

Two sides,
one room

Wax Lyrical started in 2011 as a folding table of second-hand records outside a tea stall. These days it fills a corner building in Begumpet, with bins up front and a low-lit listening bar at the back.

We buy collections big and small, clean every record by hand and grade honestly. If it skips, we tell you before you ask.

Come for a record, stay for a glass of something. The best part of a shop is the stranger who tells you what to buy.

“Buy it now, because it will not be here on Saturday.”
